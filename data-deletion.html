User Data Deletion Instructions

At Hora (Aghora Abraham Global LLC), we respect your control over your personal data.

How to Delete Your Account and Stored Data:

  1. Log into your account at Hora Web App.
  2. Navigate to Account Settings.
  3. Click "Delete Account and Erase Stored Data".

Alternatively, send an email from your registered email address to:

To: contact@bhutadamarasena.com
Subject: Data Erasure Request

Data will be permanently purged from our databases and storage clusters within 30 days.
